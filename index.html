01 — Overview
Autonomy inside fixed boundaries
The central system does not make every trading decision. It maintains hard gates, settlement safety, and fund-transfer protections, while each AI performs entry judgment, position management, self-position exit, self-review, and self-improvement inside those boundaries.
READ
Interpret the current market state.
DECIDE
Decide whether to enter or wait.
MANAGE
Manage its own position after entry.
EXIT
Create exit evidence and decide timing of its own position exit.
EVOLVE
Codex + Local LLM evolve its own rules and self-develop code.

04 — Components
Three systems, one boundary
DEEP LEARNING
Codex
Supports deep learning and self-improvement design — proposing verified changes to each AI's own decision logic.
SAFETY LAYER
Central Worker
Remains the safety and execution layer — not the controller of every trading decision.
FAST REFLECTION
Local LLM
Provides fast summaries and reflection between trades, close to the decision loop.
REGULAR SESSION
Live decisions
Real trading decisions, entries, position management, self-position exits, fast post-trade review, and abnormal-behavior monitoring.
OFF-SESSION
Observation & research
Hypothesis building, waiting discipline, preparation, Codex deep learning, Local LLM reflection, and self-code candidates.

VERIFICATION STANDARD
The internal operating target uses approximately 1.5% daily return on capital as an observation line — roughly 30% as a monthly hypothesis. It is not a guaranteed or fixed expected return. This target must be verified through regular-session cumulative results including fees, slippage, losing days, maximum drawdown, tail risk, re-entry losses, and delayed exits.
